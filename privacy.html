FREQUENCY

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Frequency iOS app (“we,” “us”). It is the policy linked from App Store Connect and from inside the app.

Who we are

Frequency is operated as its own app. Questions about this policy can be sent to getfrequency.help@gmail.com.

What we collect

Frequency asks for your first name and, optionally, a profile photo during setup. Both are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Frequency does not create an account, does not require an email address to use the app, and does not operate a user database.

Your activity in the app

Money Signs you log, lessons and Bridge Moves you complete, Circle of Five entries, Healing Tones sessions, and your Bridge Score are all stored locally on your device using standard iOS app storage. None of this activity is transmitted to a Frequency server, because Frequency does not currently operate one. We cannot see your data, and we do not sell or share it, because we never receive it in the first place.

No tracking or analytics

Frequency does not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or third-party data collection tools. Nothing about how you use the app is monitored or reported outside your device. We do not use your data for tracking across apps or websites.

Market data

The in-app ticker may request publicly available market prices from third-party services (such as Yahoo Finance and CoinGecko). Those requests do not include your name, photo, notes, or other personal content from Frequency. Those providers process the network request according to their own policies.

Payments

Subscriptions are billed and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see, receive, or store your payment card details. Apple’s own privacy policy governs the payment information you provide to them.

Photos and device permissions

If you choose to add a profile photo, Frequency uses the photo you select and stores it on your device. We do not upload that photo. You can skip the photo or remove the app to delete it.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored locally, deleting the Frequency app from your device permanently deletes your data with it. There is no separate account or server copy to remove, and there is no additional in-app account-deletion request because no account exists.

Children

Frequency is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to getfrequency.help@gmail.com.
