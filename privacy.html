FREQUENCY

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 14, 2026

What we collect

Frequency asks for your first name and, optionally, a profile photo during setup. Both are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Your activity in the app

Money Signs you log, lessons and Bridge Moves you complete, Circle of Five entries, Healing Tones sessions, and your Bridge Score are all stored locally on your device using standard iOS app storage. None of this activity is transmitted to a server, because Frequency does not currently operate one. We cannot see your data, and we do not sell or share it, because we never receive it in the first place.

No tracking or analytics

Frequency does not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or third-party data collection tools. Nothing about how you use the app is monitored or reported outside your device.

Market data

The in-app ticker may request publicly available market prices from third-party services (such as Yahoo Finance and CoinGecko). Those requests do not include your name, photo, notes, or other personal content from Frequency.

Payments

Subscriptions are billed and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see, receive, or store your payment card details. Apple’s own privacy policy governs the payment information you provide to them.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored locally, deleting the Frequency app from your device permanently deletes your data with it. There is no separate account or server copy to remove.

Children

Frequency is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for use by children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@fortresstechnologies.com.
