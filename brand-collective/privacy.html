BRAND COLLECTIVE STUDIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 30, 2026

This page is the GitHub Pages copy of the live Brand Collective Studio privacy policy. App Store Connect may use this URL or the Railway URL https://brandcollectivemedia-production.up.railway.app/legal/privacy.

Who we are

Brand Collective Studio is a native Mac application for photographers and videographers. Fortress Technologies (“we”) operates the app and the in-app studio workspace you sign into with a username and password.

What we collect

We collect the account details you provide (name, email), project and client records you create, files you upload, and App Store transaction identifiers needed to keep your subscription active.

How we use information

We use this information to operate Brand Collective Studio, keep you signed in, run your studio workspace, and keep your Mac App Store subscription active. We do not sell personal information.

Files

Files stay in your studio workspace. The Mac app uses the system Open and Save panels for local files; we only receive files you choose to upload.

Payments

Subscriptions are billed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not see, receive, or store payment card numbers. Apple’s own privacy policy governs the payment information you provide to Apple.

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the Mac app after you sign in. There is no self-serve web page for account deletion.

Sign in with your username and password. Open Settings. Choose Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm. Deletion removes your account data from the studio, including the account and the studio records stored with it (clients, projects, contracts, invoices, files, galleries, and settings). It cannot be undone.

If you subscribe through the Mac App Store, cancel the subscription in System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions as well — deleting your account does not cancel Apple billing.
