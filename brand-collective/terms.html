Agreement

Brand Collective Studio is licensed through the Mac App Store. By using the app you also agree to Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

Subscriptions and billing

Paid plans (Starter, Pro, Studio) are auto-renewable monthly subscriptions sold by Apple through StoreKit. There is no annual plan, lifetime plan, or discount tier:

  • Starter — 1 month — $9.99 USD
  • Pro — 1 month — $29.99 USD
  • Studio — 1 month — $79.99 USD

Manage or cancel in System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or from Account → Manage Subscription in the app. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

The Mac app

The Mac app is not a web browser. It loads your Brand Collective workspace inside a sandboxed native shell with StoreKit billing, native file access, and origin-locked navigation. Sign-in uses a username and password.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the client work, contracts, and files you store. Do not use the studio for unlawful content. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service.

Account deletion

After you sign in with your username and password, you may delete your account in Settings → Delete account (type DELETE to confirm). Deletion removes your account data from the studio. There is no self-serve web deletion URL. Deleting your account does not cancel Apple billing. Cancel the subscription in System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions as well.

Refunds

Mac app purchases are billed through the Mac App Store and are subject to Apple’s refund policies. To request a refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem feature.
