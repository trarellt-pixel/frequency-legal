App Support

The Brand Collective  ·  Last updated August 30, 2026

This is the App Store Connect Support URL for Brand Collective Studio, a Mac App Store app from Fortress Technologies. Railway /legal/support is a client 404. Use this GitHub Pages page, not Railway, as the Support URL.

How to get help

Email support@brandcollectivemedia.com. That is the support contact. There is no live chat. You can also use Support inside the Mac app after you sign in. Include:

  • Your account username if the issue is about sign-in or your workspace
  • Your Apple ID email if the issue is about a subscription or restore
  • Mac model and macOS version
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead

Delete your account

Account deletion is in the Mac app only. There is no self-serve web deletion URL. Sign in with your username and password. Open Settings. Choose Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm, then choose Delete my account. Deletion removes your account data from the studio. It cannot be undone.

If you subscribe through the Mac App Store, cancel the subscription in System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions as well — deleting your account does not cancel Apple billing.

Subscriptions, billing, and refunds

Starter ($9.99 / month), Pro ($29.99 / month), and Studio ($79.99 / month) are auto-renewable Mac App Store subscriptions billed by Apple through StoreKit. To cancel or change auto-renew, open System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your Mac, or Account → Manage Subscription in the app. For a refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem flow.
