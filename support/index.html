App Support

Frequency  ·  Last updated August 18, 2026

This is the support page for the Frequency iOS app. If something in the app is broken, a purchase did not unlock, or you have a question about your data, start here.

Contact us

Frequency’s support inbox is trarell.t+frequency@gmail.com. Mail goes to Frequency only. Email that address and include:

  • Your Apple ID email if the issue is about a subscription or restore
  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead

We read support email for Frequency and reply as quickly as we can, usually within two business days.

Subscriptions, billing, and refunds

Frequency Monthly ($14.99 / 1 month) and Frequency Annual ($59.99 / 1 year) are billed by Apple to your Apple ID. To cancel or change auto-renew, open iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. To restore a purchase you already paid for, use Restore Purchases in Frequency. For a refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem flow — we cannot issue App Store refunds ourselves.

Privacy and terms

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use (EULA). Frequency stores your name, optional photo, and in-app activity on your device only. Deleting the app deletes that data.

Healing Tones and lessons

Frequency content is educational and for personal development. It is not financial, investment, or medical advice. Healing Tones are wellness audio, not a medical treatment.
